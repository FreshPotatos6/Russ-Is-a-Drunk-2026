Week 5

NFL Doppelgängers

It is now Week 5 and teams in both our league and in the NFL are starting to show who they really are. This is the last time all NFL teams will have played the same amount of games as our teams in fantasy so I have decided to compare each fantasy team with the NFL team that has the same record. These comparisons are about how the teams have looked, what the teams rely on, and the players themselves. I may or may not have also thrown in which person each of you reminds me of on the corresponding team as well. If you like the comparisons then I feel great, if you are insulted by them then I feel even better.

Note: All of these comparisons were written before the Thursday game so it does not take into account The Bucs big win, but maybe it is a sign that Abi will also be getting her first win this week too...

Jeff: Smo's Squad
NFL Team: San Francisco 49ers (4-0)
Comparison Analysis

Jeff and the 49ers are both undefeated and look dominant while doing it. I would guess that Jeff also lives next to a substation because his players are getting hurt at a rate similar to that of the 49ers players. Luckily for both teams, their true studs are healthy and delivering in a big way. Gibbs is a front-runner for fantasy MVP and Brock Purdy is a front-runner for NFL MVP. The real concern for both teams is that they are on the verge of starting players who may not be legitimate starters. All of these similarities do make me question Jeff as a human, though. Jeff has always seemed like a stand-up guy, but so did Shanahan until the real story of his car crash came out... when will the real story about Jeff come out?

Brad: Hampton Inn
NFL Team: Minnesota Vikings (4-0)
Comparison Analysis

Comparing Brad to the Vikings may seem like an insult to Brad at first, but I promise it's not! Both teams are more than happy to get rid of UofM players and they are loaded with talent. The receiving corps is the strength of both teams, with a superstar receiver leading the way. However, the talent of both squads is sometimes overlooked due to a glaring issue on their roster. For the Vikings, that issue is QB play; for Brad, it is target share for his WRs. Is the success sustainable for either team? The real concern is leadership getting cute. KOC is known for getting too cute with his play-calling and Brad has an inflated ego because his wife is always saying how cute he is... I just don't see it.

Karolyn: Not Scott's Brother
NFL Team: Seattle Seahawks (3-1)
Comparison Analysis

Both teams came into the year with high expectations; the Seahawks are the defending champs and Karolyn had a great draft. Somehow JSN fell to both teams and is absolutely carrying them despite the fact that their highly touted defenses have slightly underwhelmed. They have looked like dominant teams minus one hiccup of a game. The QB play for each has some question marks around their actual ability, but they are in great systems that cover their weaknesses. The issue with both teams is that they may have the worst running backs in the league. Jadarian Price looks like a bust of a draft pick for the Seahawks and seems as if he plays with his eyes closed, which is another tie-in to Karolyn—because I learned this summer that Karolyn closes her eyes in volleyball when passing and hitting the ball.

Nick: Taylor's Cardigan
NFL Team: New York Giants (3-1)
Comparison Analysis

Nick moved on to his backup QB after Week 1 and so did the Giants. On top of that, neither team has much going on through the air, with Nick having the worst WRs in the league and the Giants playing without a real starting QB. Somehow, both teams are still off to a 3-1 start. You may think that's due to a dominant run game, but that is not quite true either. The RBs for both teams have been solid, but nothing special. The Giants are 3-1 with a barely positive point differential and the highest-rated "luck" score in the league. Nick's team is somehow 3-1 with the 8th-most points in the league, so I am arbitrarily handing him the highest "luck" score, too. It feels unsustainable for both. The real match for these two, however, is Cam Skattebo and Nick himself: they both have the exact same IQ score.

Cody: West Canaan Coyotes
NFL Team: Pittsburgh Steelers (2-2)
Comparison Analysis

I hate to do this to Cody because I know he hates Aaron Rodgers, but his team and the Steelers are eerily similar. Each team is 2-2 and you really aren't sure how high their ceiling is, but you know they have a high floor. Both teams rely heavily on their strong RB duo and have some veteran WRs that are inconsistent at best. On top of that, they both start a garbage QB I would never want on my team. Cody has George Kittle, who is a stud at TE, and the Steelers use the TE position a ton, too. Where Cody and the Steelers are really similar is between him and DK Metcalf. Both DK and Cody view themselves as physical juggernauts, even if the numbers say otherwise. Cody joined my volleyball team and claimed he was most confident about two things in life: his running skills and his volleyball skills. He was not invited back after the season and blamed his poor play on a supposed "injury."

Ross: R's Remarkable Team
NFL Team: Los Angeles Rams (2-2)
Comparison Analysis

Ross is the defending champ and the Rams were the preseason favorites, so expectations were sky-high for both teams. Neither team's record is anything special, but both have flashed potential brilliance, showing they could still live up to the hype. Both teams have Kyren Williams at RB with a solid backup behind him, both rely on their incredible but fragile WR, and both have a pocket passer that can pile on points at any time. The defense for both squads has been disappointing so far and there are major questions about their passing games if their star WR gets hurt. Puka Nacua on the Rams reminds me of Ross. Both of them seem like nice, friendly dudes, but then you hear or see them in different situations and they're maniacs. Luckily for Ross, most of his maniac-ness happens on the soccer field, whereas Puka's is off the field—but they're still both maniacs nonetheless.

Rob: Little Chocolate Donuts.
NFL Team: Philadelphia Eagles (2-2)
Comparison Analysis

Both of these teams came out of the gate hot with a 2-0 record and people were calling them championship favorites. Then, in the last two games, both teams look like they are falling apart. The question is: which version more accurately matches these teams? Both teams rely heavily on their running game and have a completely untrustworthy receiving corps, with their best receiving option being an aging TE. Each team is unafraid to make moves during the season to and each has already completed a significant trade this year. The real similarity between the two is Vic Fangio, the Eagles' DC. Both Fangio and Rob are football savants, but they also both have had limited success when put in charge of a team.

Dan: Squirtle Squad
NFL Team: Cincinnati Bengals (2-2)
Comparison Analysis

When Dan got off to a 2-0 start, the league took notice and thought his team was for real; the same can be said for the Bengals. Over the last two weeks, however, both teams have looked significantly worse. They each rely heavily on their star QB and their star WR, who has been struggling with injuries this year. The star WR being injured played a role in their losses and both teams have questions about their ability to run the ball. They each also have a TE who can absolutely go off one week and then get zero catches the next. Dan has the most in common with Joe Burrow. Both Dan and Joe have tried changing their hairstyle to hide from their celebrity look-a-like, but Joe Burrow will forever be Macaulay Culkin and Dan will forever be Napoleon Dynamite.

Luke: Brett Mahomes
NFL Team: New Orleans Saints (1-3)

The biggest similarity between the Saints and me is Spencer Rattler. Spencer Rattler is the most annoying QB in the league with all the nonsense he spews and I am the most annoying owner in this league with all the trade offers I send.

Jon: Used 2001 Honda Civic
NFL Team: Washington Commanders (1-3)
Comparison Analysis

Comparison Analysis
