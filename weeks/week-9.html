Week 9

The Luck Index

Here we are, it is week 9 and now is the time for us to determine who is a contender and who is a pretender. The best way to see that is to look at how lucky each team has been. I broke each team’s success down and compared it to 4 criteria that should indicate success; Points Scored, All-Play Win %, Projected Wins, and Close Game Success. All of those scores are then compared using standard deviation to create an all-encompassing luck score. The luck score is just based on wins/losses, so it is impossible for Jeff’s team to have any bad luck or for Chris to have any good luck. I know people are going to complain that injury luck is not included but I couldn’t figure out a simple way to value it so lets just pretend injuries are a skill issue and not a luck issue. The four categories are explained below.

  • Points Scored: This simply compares where the team ranks in wins compared with where they rank in points scored.
  • All-Play Win %: This category compares their actual win % with what their win % would be if they played every single team every single week.
  • Projected Wins: This compares how many wins a team has compared to how many wins they are projected to have. Projected wins takes how each week has gone and places a value on how likely that score is to get the team a win.
  • Close Game Success: This looks at how a team does in close games and competitive games. If they win more close games then they get a positive score and if they lose then they get a negative score. The closer the game the more weight it carries.
