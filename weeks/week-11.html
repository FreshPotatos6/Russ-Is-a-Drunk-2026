Week 11

Playoff Odds

Two-thirds of the season is now done and the playoff picture is taking shape. 11 of the 12 teams are still technically alive in this hunt, even if just barely. This week I decided to give everyone their playoff odds based purely on my feelings and no actual data. Is my gut right? I guess we will find out. I also included a pop culture quote that aligns with your team. Check out if your team truly has a shot at the playoffs or if you are just a waste of space.

Playoff Odds 0%

Unfortunately for Chris he has found himself without a shot at making the playoffs, but he is still here putting in the work, and that is respectable. The lesson learned? Never put your trust in the Eagles. The real fine Chris is trying to avoid is embarrassment from a winless season.
