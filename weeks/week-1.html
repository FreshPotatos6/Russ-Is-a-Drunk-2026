Week 1

Backyard Football Draft Grades

As a kid I spent hundreds of hours playing backyard sports games on my computer, I loved these games and I thought it would be fun to compare each team's draft to a backyard kid. These comparisons are strictly on the draft and the kid's personality/skills any comparisons to appearance are purely coincidental. Every backyard kid is somebody's favorite, but every kid is also somebody's least favorite so if you hate who I assigned you... too bad! If you don't know the backyard kids then I feel bad for you because these were the most fun games. Also, special shoutout to Vicki Kawaguchi who was one of my favorites but I didn't feel like she fit anyone's draft.
