Week 4

League History

This week I want to get everyone acquainted with each other just a little bit more and give a little intro into each team; their highlights, their willingness to trade, their loyalty to players, etc. I also thought it would be important for everyone to be able to put a name with a face so I have included photos for each person. Hopefully this tells you a little about each manager and mostly I am hoping that it will encourage all of you to start making some trades with one another!

This league started at Bdubs in Howell in 2013 and got its name when Russ got absolutely hammered and had to be driven home. Russ has been out of the league and sober for years now but he likes that we still use the league name. We are now in our 14th year and half of the league has been playing since at least 2014. This also is the first season where we have the same 12 teams for the year prior. Through the years we have had some horrible takes (like me being convinced that Tavon Austin was going to be a thing) and some incredible calls (like when Brad called out the breakout of then unknown Ravens TE Mark Andrews and picking him in the 13th round). Here is a little about everyone in the league and their history since they have joined.

Chris: Chris's Crazy Team
Years 14

Best Keeper Alshon Jeffery
Best Season 2024
Franchise History

Chris has been in this league since its inception and in 2024 he finally won his first championship and promptly followed that up with a last place finish last year. He holds the distinction of being the first person to lose over 100 games in the league but only 3 teams have made the playoffs more than him. He is the definition of hot or cold and has finished the season in every place in the league except 6th and 7th, the two middle places. His best season came in 2024 when he dominated the regular season en route to a 13- 2 record, led the league in points scored, and won the championship; making him just the third triple crown winner in the league. Chris is usually willing to talk trades, but almost never actually trades. His loyalty should be questioned because he is tied for the 2nd most different keepers in league history and the longest he has kept a player is 4 years. The player he kept the longest was Alshon Jeffery, if I kept Alshon for 4 straight years I would probably be jaded too.

Cody is another OG although he did try quitting a couple of years ago. Including this season Cody has won exactly half of his games. Him and Dan are in a tight race to become just the third person to win 100 games in the league. Cody is tied for the most years in the league without coming in last place but also holds the record for most years in the league without a championship. His best season was way back in 2015 when went 12-2 and entered the playoffs as the top seed only to get upset by the leagues namesake, Russ Gavin. In Cody's defense that season was marred with in league collusion by the would be champion. Cody will tell you that he hates trading but if you make him a fair offer he is actually open to it. He is very picky about players he likes and his reasoning for liking or hating players often contradicts itself. Cody struggles with loyalty in keeping players he is tied for the 2nd most different keepers in league history and his most kept player is Michael Thomas at just 4 years, although he has kept Jaylen Waddle for 3 years now but it is split in two separate stints, twice in the 9th round and now once in the 5th.

Luke: Brett Mahomes
Years 14
Record 125-62
Playoffs 10
Titles 3
Trades 41
Best Keeper Jamaar Chase
Best Season 2018
Franchise History

I am and always have been the commissioner of the league, all of you are here because of me! (except Jeff who Cody conned into joining). My proudest accomplishment is starting this league as a keeper league where you keep the player in the round you drafted them in. When I came up with the idea in the back room at Under Armour I thought it was genius and I was correct. There are other leagues that also do it now but I like to claim that I was the first and am trying to get credit in the Fantasy Football hall of fame. I have wont the triple crown twice in this league but my best season I did not even win the championship. In 2018 I completed the only perfect regular season in league history only to get beaten in the finals by Dan. My favorite season however was 2015 when I started off 1-4 and came storming back to make the playoffs. If you didn't know, I love trading. It is my favorite part of fantasy football and have considered forcing every team to trade at least once every season. If you want to trade I will find something! I am loyal to my players and am one of only 3 teams to have less keepers than years in the league. My longest kept player is Ja'Marr Chase at 5 years finally passing my beloved yet failed keeper from the 2010s Lamar Miller.

Ross is also one of the original league members and also the hardest member to find a picture of. Ross has had some of the wildest draft picks in league history, most notably in 2015 and 2016 where he drafted Doug Martin and Thomas Rawls with each first round pick. Those two put up a combined 600 yards and each averaged under 3 yards a carry. In spite of this Ross is one of only two members of the 100 win club, has been to the playoffs second most in league history, and last year finally won his first championship. Last year might be his championship season but our first season, 2013, was his best season. In 2013 he went 11-3 but lost in the inaugural title game. Ross typically does not trade and takes a "wait and see" attitude to trade offers, hopefully that changes. Ross is arguably the most loyal member of the league. Not only has he had the longest kept player in Aaron Jones at 6 years, but he is also one of only three people to have less keepers than years in the league.

Dan is an original member of this league but did take a year off to focus on his studies. He is one of only two players to win multiple championships, has the second highest postseason rate of anyone, and is knocking on the door to becoming just the third member of the 100 win club. In high school Dan and I were in a fantasy football league with our JV soccer coach and he also won the championship that season. Despite having two championships Dan's best season was a year he lost in the playoffs. In 2019 Dan went 10-4 while leading the league in points scored but got upset in the semifinals and ended up in 3rd. Dan has made a decent amount of trades in this league but most of those were in his wild single days. Now that he is a family man he is much less prone to trading. Dan's most kept player was Antonio Brown who he kept 4 straight years. Antonio Brown was this league's first truly dominant keeper and Dan actually felt bad about having such a big advantage.

Rob joined the league in year 2 when it expanded to 12 teams and has not looked back. Rob knows the most about football in the league but he definitely prefers proven veterans to young players. He is in the top half of the league in total wins but has never been able to come home with the championship. Rob has some of the worst luck in the league, just last season he was 4th in the league in points and finished in 11th place while the team with the 7th most points made the playoffs. I credit Rob with helping me get started with fantasy football from him showing me his teams when I was just a teenager. Rob's best season was 2022 when he finished 3rd in points scored and 3rd in point differential. He is a willing trader and has won some of the most lopsided trades in league history. Rob is probably the least loyal keeper in this league. Nobody has kept as many different players as him and the longest he kept anyone for was Patrick Mahomes for just 3 seasons.

Nick is a year away from achieving 10 years in the league, he has one of the highest win percentages in the league and is 3rd all time in point differential per season. On top of that Nick has some of the best luck in the league; he consistently ends up with a better record than his points scored would indicate. Nick was one of the first league members to also not care about the QB he drafted but has sense reversed on this and started picking QBs quite early. Nick's best season was in 2022 where he went 11-4, won the championship, and came in 2nd in points scored... just missing out on a triple crown season. He is one of three league members I consider trade friendly. He is always looking for trades no matter how good or bad his team is and is willing to trade picks on top of players. He always seems to have good keepers but his best keeper was also his longest tenured - Nick Chubb. He had Nick Chubb for 4 seasons in the 16th round and would have had him for longer had it not been for an awful injury.

Jeff is willing to talk trades but rarely pulls the trigger, I was shocked to see how few trades he has made because we have trade discussions multiple times a year. He is loyal to at least one player, Justin Jefferson, who he has kept every year he has been in the league. The last three years both his keepers have remained unchanged with Gibbs and Jefferson.
