Week 3

Team Trends

Alright guys, we are 2 weeks into the season, and I figured it was time to review how every team looks now compared to when their team was drafted. I am going to do these reports throughout the year to show how your team is trending compared to the last time. Trending up doesn’t necessarily mean your team is great in that area, just that it has improved. Likewise trending down does not mean it is bad, just that it has gotten worse. I have also included the standouts for each team in positive and negative ways, as well as included a player who seems to be a bit of a wild card. At the bottom of the page is a graphic summarizing how all teams look across the board. Let’s have a great week 3!
