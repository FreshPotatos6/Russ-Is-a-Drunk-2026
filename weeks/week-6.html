Week 6

Tarot Card Readings

It is week 6 and there is just so much unknown in the fantasy football world, so I decided to take out some tarot cards and see what the future has in store for all of us. For those that do not know (which was me until an hour or so ago) there are 22 main tarot cards. Each tarot card has a meaning, or a range of meanings. Tarot cards do not tell you what has happened but instead what is to come. The orientation in which the card is drawn changes the meaning completely. Reversed cards can be "uprighted" by doing things on the upright side and upright cards can be "reversed" by doing things on the reversed side. This means that while tarot cards do tell the future it is a future that can be altered. Without further ado here is what everyone's future holds...

Quick note: In fear of accidently calling demonic forces I did not actually spiritually draw tarot cards, these readings were done safely and spirit free.

Ross: R's Remarkable Team
Record: 3-2
I. The Magician ▼ REVERSED
Drawn Reversed:
Manipulation • Unfulfilled potential • Illusion
Upright Meaning: Manifestation • Skill • Resourcefulness
Tarot Reading

Ross and his team look like a true master magician, but his Reversed Magician card says that he may be nothing more than a sidewalk trickster. The reversed Magician warns Ross of his team's success being an illusion, that the early success is manipulating Ross into believing in players he should not believe in, and warns of players on his roster who cannot fulfil the potential he once saw in them. Kyren Williams, Chirs Olave, Brock Purdy, and Jaylen Warren are all off to much better starts than anticipated this is creating an illusion of them being elite, however backup RBs on their own team threaten Warren and William's success, Olave is still injury prone, and Purdy has shown the ability to lay an egg at any time. Ross also has players like Tuten, Burden, Hampton, and Fields that ooze potential- the Magician claims that may remain unfulfilled potential. Has Ross been manipulated to believe his team is great as-is? To escape this fate Ross must use his skill and resourcefulness to bolster his roster and manifest wins.

Rob: Little Chocolate Donuts.
Record: 3-2
V. The Hierophant ▲ UPRIGHT
Drawn Upright:
Tradition • Conformity • Belief systems
Reversed Meaning: Rebellion • Unconventionality • New approaches
Tarot Reading

They say "tradition is not the worship of ashes, but the preservation of fire", will rob preserve his ashes or keep his fire? The Hierophant is a traditionalist and it claims that Rob will stick with his belief systems, conform to what he knows, and stick with what has traditionally worked for him and his team. Over years in the league it has shown that Rob's belief system centers around the rostering known commodities and his roster reflects this by having old guys all over the place. Travis Kelce, Matt Stafford, Davante Adams, Christian McCaffrey, and Alvin Kamara are all considered "Uncs" in the locker room giving a dependable floor but also a lower ceiling. Rob likes guys that conform to their positions norm, which is probably why he is still rostering big armed Justin Herbert in spite of Herbert's atrocious start. Rob believes in these traditions, but the Hierophant warns that these traditions may need to be broken to make something truly special. Can Rob break some of his traditions to give his team the chance to be truly special? If Rob can buck tradition and instead take new unconventional approaches by rebelling against what has been and reaching for what might be, his team could truly compete for a title. He has shown some signs of this by holding onto Harrison Jr. and signing Tre Harris, but a bigger splash is needed.

Record: 0-5

Drawn Upright:
Harmony • Good Choices • Alignment

We have all seen how Chris' bad choices in the draft has led to imbalance and disharmony with his team, hence the 0-5 start, but his Lovers card is upright signaling better times ahead. Luckily for Chris most of his players still have value, he just needs to make a few good choices in who he moves them for. The Lovers show that with the right roster alignment Chris can still make the playoffs.

Nick: Taylor's Cardigan
Record: 3-2

Drawn Reversed:
Lack of control • Directionless • Aggression
Upright Meaning: Determination • Action • Victory
Tarot Reading

Nick's chariot awaits, but where it leads… nobody knows. Drawing a reversed Chariot card is a scary endeavor, it warns that directionless aggression can lead to a complete lack of control for his team, and could completely derail his season. Nick has been crazy aggressive with his FAB spending this year, he has already spent nearly half of his budget. The confusing part about his spending is that it seems completely directionless, he has spent nearly all of his FAB on RBs and then sat them on his bench. Ignoring players Nick got via FAB bids, he had a healthy Ashton Jeanty and Cam Skattebo as starters, while his bench has a few banged up players; Breece Hall, Kyle Monangai, and Rico Dowdle. Dan: Squirtle Squad
Record: 3-2
X. Wheel of Fortune ▼ REVERSED
Drawn Reversed:
Bad luck • Resistance to change • Setbacks
Upright Meaning: Good luck • Cycles • Turning points
Tarot Reading

If "a great fortune in the hands of a fool is a great misfortune", will Dan prove himself a fool? 5 weeks into the season Dan has had great fortune, he is tied for 4th place despite being 9th in points scored. Dan pulling the reversed Wheel of Fortune card may show that the tides are turning against him. the reversed fortune card warns of bad luck, setbacks, and of a person that is resistant to change. The card knows Dan. Sports data analysts will tell you that luck always evens out and that would mean that there is bad luck coming Dan's way. Fortune however is not all about luck but also about how you handle setbacks, Dan has already encountered some setbacks, but more appear to be coming. Will Harrison eat into Nico Collins' massive target share? Will the Buccaneers bad offense drag Bucky Irving down with them? Can Josh Allen continue his torrid pace with tougher defenses ahead? Dan's resistance to change may be his biggest obstacle, he has only made 2 moves this entire season and he may need change to succeed. If Dan can cycle through the right players, make good moves at vital turning points, and get some good luck then he may be able to upright his Wheel of Fortune card and become a true title threat.

Drawn Upright:
Truth • Cause & effect • Accountability

Tarot Reading

Justice is more than just the store Cody frequented in high school to pick up girls, it is also the tarot card he drew. The Justice card claims that if Cody can embrace the truth and be accountable then the cause and effect will be more wins for his team. He is currently 2-3 because he has failed to do those things. Right off the bat this year Cody fought the truth by not admitting what a great draft I had for him! Aaron Jones, Garrett Wilson, George Kittle, and Derrick Henry have all far exceeded expectations and I drafted all of them. On top of that his slow start is somewhat due to his lack of accountability. Cody decided to use one of his keepers on Jaylen Waddle, who has been an unmitigated disaster. The cause & effect is simple; if Cody trusts guys just because he like them, his season won't get better. If he trusts the guys the data supports then he can quickly turn his season around. Luckily for Cody the cards believe he will do the right thing and make a playoff push. However, if he continues to blame injustice, not take accountability, and be dishonest with himself about what is going wrong then there may truly be no fixing it.

On the other hand, if the temptation to hold on to his highly skilled but hurt players causes him to be attached to those players then he will continue to struggle with roster restrictions and will continue to find victories hard to come by.

Jeff: Smo's Squad
Record: 5-0
XXI. The World ▼ REVERSED
Drawn Reversed:
Incompletion • Lack of closure • Shortcuts
Upright Meaning: Completion • Integration • Accomplishment
Tarot Reading

Jeff appears to have the whole world in his hands, but will he capture it or just let it slip? The reversed World is a tough draw, it says Jeff's team will take shortcuts throughout the year, find that too many incompletions lose him too many points, and results in a lack of championship closure by the end of the year. On paper Jeff's team is great, he is leading the league in scoring, set a record for points scored, and is the only undefeated team still standing. What could go wrong? Well, the reversed World warns about the possibilities and Jeff's team may be falling right into what it says. The biggest issue with Jeff's team is the RB position and he had no QB depth, instead of making trades to fix these problems he took a shortcut by overpaying for waivers to fill those gaps. Nobody believes that Kendre Miller is a season long solution and D'Andre Swift has a lot of question marks about usage and health. Another issue for Jeff's team is his WR catch %. Jeff has loaded his team with big play WRs, the risk with his guys is that they don't get a ton of targets, rely on big plays, and have a history of drops. Could incompletions on big plays come back to haunt Jeff? His team has set the bar so high this year that anything but a championship will feel like a lack of closure. If Jeff can integrate actual solutions into his team he could find himself completing the season with the accomplishment of being the champion.
